PAUSED · HOW TO PLAY

        

        
One life: rear hits eliminate. Last cart standing wins the round. Reach the win target to win the match. Solo practice has no scoring.

        
Yellow rail drops: SPD = +25% speed. Pink rail drops: HIT = hits eliminate from any side. Both last 10 seconds; remaining seconds appear beside each cart's score. IN / OUT shows who is still in the round.

        
Phone: swipe to steer at junctions; tap to shoot. Keyboard: WASD / Space, or arrows / Right Ctrl, to steer / shoot. Gamepad: stick / D-pad to steer, A to shoot. Escape / B resumes; Start selects. R or Start restarts a finished match.
